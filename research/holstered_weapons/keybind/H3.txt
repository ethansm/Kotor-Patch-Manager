3. SAFE TO CALL EVERY FRAME? (answer: no; throttle)
=====================================================================
- Cost (O, not measured; no timing in repo): linear in the number of objects in self's area, with the X-gap prune. Each candidate that passes the cheap tests and is closer than best costs one LOS ray. Repo guidance: "at most once per action end, not per frame" (mods678_research/ai_review_2026-10-01/designs/design_ai_engine_fixes.md:203). CompanionAI calls it only on A1 spin events (CompanionAI.cpp:997).
- Side effects (V for the 0x57C990 body): none to game state. The body writes only stack locals. Callees: GetArea, GetGameObject, 0x5E1680, 0x5D1750, 0x4452B0, 0x5142F0 read-only (by the decompile). 0x0052BB60 (LOS, 767 B, __thiscall RET 0x20) calls 0x0083EA60 (CExoArrayList::Add) and 0x0084A640. These were NOT traced (O).
- Perception lag (V from design doc:204, O for the PC): the "seen" test uses self's perception list. Companions refresh perception only about every 4 s (P2). A newly engaged enemy may be missed for seconds. A just-dead enemy is excluded at once (filter 3).
- Recommendation (G): call at most every 250-500 ms from the holster tick, and only while the holster state needs a stow decision. The holster delay is 1.5 s, so this granularity is enough.
