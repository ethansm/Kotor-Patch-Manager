Edge cases to handle:
- w <= 0 (behind camera / at near plane): do not project endpoints separately; clip the segment or hide that axis.
- Foreshortened axis (projected length < 10 px): direction undefined; hide handle, disable hover and drag for it.
- Yaw 180 (HolsterEditor.cpp:700 flipYaw): projection mirrors automatically; existing RMB NudgeWorld mapping (:946-950) will disagree with it.
- Handle clicks also start view drag: insideView is a raw cursor test (HolsterEditor.cpp:845, :976). Buttons on top do not block it; needs a suppress rule.
- gaveUp latch (ModConfig.cpp:271): a handle the engine re-lays out 8 times freezes silently until page re-enter.
- Pane resize / ultrawide: recompute view rect per frame via PaneRect(VIEW), not once at LAYOUT.
- No hide flag: editor parks items at (-9999,-9999) (HolsterEditor.cpp:481). Reuse that pattern.
- Y sign unproven (23_menu_3d_shared_migration.md:146): a wrong sign mirrors handles; needs one live probe.

Testability (claim 4 of brief): YES for the math. Put in HseView.h as pure helpers: worldToScreen(float vp[16], p[3], HseRect view, int flipY, float out[2]) with w guard; nearestSegment2D(mouse, s0, s1); projectDelta(dx, dy, vx, vy, minLen). Matrix source is the blocker, not the math.

Recommended order (not built): 1) live probe logging camerahook and mount world pose plus extent (no projection); 2) confirm camera rule and Y sign; 3) only then add handles.
