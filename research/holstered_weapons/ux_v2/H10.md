## UX ideas Gemini missed (real sources)
1. Blender numeric input during transform: press G/R/S, type a value; G then X/Y/Z constrains the axis. https://wiki.blender.jp/Doc:2.6/Manual/3D_interaction/Transform_Control/Numeric_Input
2. Blender precision: hold Shift for 1/10 speed, Ctrl snaps, releasing a modifier mid-drag reverts. https://docs.blender.org/manual/ru/3.1/scene_layout/object/editing/transform/control/precision.html
3. Gimbal lock: Anamnesis warns Euler X/Y/Z sliders can gimbal-lock while the gizmo stays exact; uniform-scale lock toggle. https://github.com/imchillin/Anamnesis/wiki/Rotating%2C-Scaling%2C-and-Positioning
4. libigl: W/E/R hotkeys switch gizmo mode, gizmo anchored at the centroid. https://libigl.github.io/tutorial/
5. ImGui: Ctrl+click types an exact value; ImGuiSliderFlags_AlwaysClamp enforces range bounds (search snippet, verify on master). https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ocornut/imgui/master/imgui_widgets.cpp
