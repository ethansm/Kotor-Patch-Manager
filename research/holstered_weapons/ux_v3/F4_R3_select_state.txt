Implications (derived, not tested)
- Copying the hover tint into the normal border (+0x98..0xA0 <- +0x110..0x118) reproduces the colour only. Corner/edge art (+0xDC/+0xE0 vs +0x154/+0x158) stays normal-state art unless written by a new helper.
- If bit0 stays clear (SetActive(0)), the normal border is drawn, so a permanent hover look needs the normal border's images changed too, not just its tint.
- A button text colour needs a button-aware gate on +0x160 (see Q1 derived offsets) plus confirmation that 0x417140 works on ctl+0x178.
