- K2MenuUiApi.Control(page,item) ModConfigApi.h:116 returns the raw ctl (ModConfig.cpp:1158-1162). Holster editor uses it for hit tests (HolsterEditor.cpp:1557).

  button_clone=1 (mod_config.ini:8, default; ModConfig.cpp:201-215), else a label clone (fallback :221).
- PITFALL A: CSWGuiButton keeps its border at +0x70 (normal) and +0xE8 (highlight), not +0x60 (GuiButton.h:24-26; doc 08_shield_bar.md:500).
  guikit::setFill / setAlpha / setBorderTint gate on +0x60 and return false, with no log, on a button.
  The equipment mod logs exactly this case (EquipCharPreview.cpp:309-311).
- PITFALL B: K2MenuUiApi does not say whether an item is a button or a label clone. Check the ctl vtable (ButtonVtable vs LabelVtable) yourself.
- Re-apply: ModConfig.cpp re-applies only the RECT (applyOne :277-297, kMaxReapply 8 at :68) and calls the entry tick (:1322).
  No fill, tint or style writes in ModConfig.cpp. A fill set by the holster editor persists.
  Hover uses the +0xE8 highlight border, so both states need the fill.
- PITFALL C: item cap. HseView.h:790-793 kPageItemsBase 26 + kInspItems 12 = 38 of 40 (static_assert).
  An overlay label (gear pattern: click-through AddCustom over the button) adds 6 items = 44 > 40, refused.
- Text: "R" must be cleared with guibutton::setButtonText(btn,"") (GuiButton.h:102-112) once the arrow is the fill.
- Target size: reset slot width max(18, slot.h), at most a third of the row (HseView.h:1017-1025). A 32x32 source is enough.
