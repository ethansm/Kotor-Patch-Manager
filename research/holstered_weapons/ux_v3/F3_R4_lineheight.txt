== 5. Computation
- Assumes font(+4) = 0.16, this+0x54 = 2.0, output in px.
- h = 0.16 * 2.0 * 100.0 = 32.0 px at 3840x1600.
- Cell cross-check: 0.16 * 100 = 16 texels (one 16x16 glyph cell). scripts/character_page_mockup.py:80 uses TEXT_K 0.75; times SY = 1600/600 = 2.667 gives 2.0 px/texel (:13). 16 texels * 2.0 px = 32 px. Consistent.
- Measured cap heights (F2_H3_screens.txt): button captions 14-15 px, label caps 18 px. A 9-texel cap at 2.0 px/texel = 18 px. This supports 32 px over 16 px (a 16 px line would give a cap near 9 px).
- Alternative A: this+0x54 stays 1.0, so h = 16.0 px.
- Alternative B: if the scale were SY = 8/3, h = 42.7 px. This looks like the source of the "~42-unit font cell" in F2_H1 sec. 3. 00479ab0.c gives 2.0, not 2.667, so 42.7 is not the expected value.
