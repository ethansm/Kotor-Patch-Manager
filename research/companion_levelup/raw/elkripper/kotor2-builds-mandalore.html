KOTOR 2 - Mandalore Builds

This file details some considerations to make for a Mandalore build, and may eventually contain a few Mandalore builds from different playthroughs. We'll see how far I take it.

======================

Continuing the tradition of KOTOR I, Canderous comes with stats suitable for melee combat and feats mostly for ranged combat. Despite him coming with a ranged weapon, a lot of builds use him as melee.
He begins as, and remains, a Soldier.

Soldier Class Skills: Demolitions, Awareness, Treat Injury

======================

Notes:
  • Dialog tree
  • Obtained on Duxn
  • Has companion quest to find Mandalorians on different planets
  • Can create simulants for you
  • Influence gained/lost only by your actions when he is in your party
  • Not much point anyway, you don't get anything from him and there aren't many opportunities anyway
  • It is mathematically impossible to lose enough influence to fully minimize.
  • There are no Light-Side ways to gain influence
  • Influence locations:
    • Anywhere
      • Kill innocents
    • Nar Shaddaa
      • Support Mandalore in bringing the clans together (unconfirmed)
    • Dantooine
      • Support Mandalore in bringing the clans together (unconfirmed)
      • Kill Akkere
    • Onderon
      • Side with rabble rouser, support him in calling for an end to the monarchy.
      • After that, talk to Anda, agree to remove the guard captains, talk to Madalore and tell him Vaklu is better
      • When the riot starts, tell Mandalore that if you want Vaklu's support, you have to help him out
    • Onderon Palace
      • If you side with the Queen, after Vaklu is defeated agree to kill him (and do it)
    • Ravager
      • Kill Tobin

This is a basic melee-focused build. It prioritizes survivability over damage output.

A damage-oriented build would probably take most of the same feats, but in a different order (although it'd probably take the Two-Weapon line instead of Dueling). It'd probably also push STR before CON.

The last two feats can be taken situationally.
