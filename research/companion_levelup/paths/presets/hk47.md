## Open questions
1. Live HK lacks Power Blast and has Sniper Shot + Improved Sniper Shot by design (mod #8); only open point is whether Modified/Integrated Assassin Protocols arrive at 11/17 (not modelled).
2. Is Dueling really active with a single pistol while the other hand is empty/off (feat text says ranged too)? `ranged_single` depends on it.
3. Is Weapon Specialization granted by TSLRCM at 12 (elkripper table says "s" selected)? Presets select it manually.
4. Skill points are proposals (Security >= 15 on Hard for the Droid Factory is covered by combat/tech/tank by ~L20; combat preset has 10 Security around L14).
5. DEX bumps from elkripper assume start DEX16; live DEX is 14 so +1 steps give 15-20. details.txt hints the level-8 bonus may go to CON (12) instead of DEX; the engine's fixed per-companion attribute gains are not modelled and only matter to DEX-gated feats (none picked).
6. Max level 30; Precise Shot IV/V (min char 16/20) and the L29/L30 slots are low value, fine to swap.
7. Equipment (rifle vs pistols, Keen/crystal upgrades, implants) is outside the level-up mod; no preset enforces the weapon.
