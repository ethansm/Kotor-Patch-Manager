## Sources

Computer Use 9/15 gives INT +1 each (+250/500 XP), Repair 9/15/21 gives CON +1, DEX +1, DEX+CON +1; last dialogue (Influence <10 or >90, after both CU 15 and Repair 21 steps) gives WIS +1, +10 max FP, Moving Meditation, 1750 XP. These gates drive the skill plans.

- `raw/web/t3m4_starwarskotor.html` -> `text/t3m4_starwarskotor.txt`: starwarskotor.com guide is for KOTOR 1 (INT 20 at level 3, different feats); used only for general ideas (Gear Head line, Weapon Focus pistol), NOT stats.

Plans reach both as early as the cap allows. Repair 21 also counts Gear Head bonus? (unverified in-game whether the dialogue check uses base rank or total.)

3. Is `single` as a wizard style for a pistol-wielder acceptable, or should it be `pistols` only? (Dueling is not selectable for droids.)

5. Max level 30 (elkripper stops at 30, consistent).
