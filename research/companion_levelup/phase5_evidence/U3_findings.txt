## 0x656710 LoadFromSave and the 0x656758 capture
- `0x656735 mov [ebp-0x1C],ecx` (table), `0x656750 mov ecx,[ebp-0x1C]`, `0x656753 call 0x6564C0`, **0x656758 `89 45 F0` (mov [ebp-0x10],eax) + `C7 45 FC FF FF FF FF` (mov [ebp-4],-1)** = 10 bytes / 2 whole instructions (U3_raw_disasm.txt:~66-75). - A "5-byte" hook cannot cover these 3+7 bytes exactly: the hook must take BOTH instructions (10 bytes, as S1 said) -- OR hook the second instruction alone: **0x65675B `C7 45 FC FF FF FF FF` (7 B, one instruction)**, params `[ebp-0x1c]` + `eax` (EAX still holds the result, nothing clobbers it between). Prefer 0x65675B (single instruction, simpler relocation); capture still unproven live (framework param read at this site = the pending log-only probe).
