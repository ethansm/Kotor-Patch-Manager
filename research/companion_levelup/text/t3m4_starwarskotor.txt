Star Wars Knights of the Old Republic - T3-M4 Build
Half of the fun of Knights of the Old Republic is developing your party members in your own individual way, and I encourage you to do so. Nonetheless, I am providing development guides for every playable character as I have figured out ways to develop a very powerful party, and if you're the type of person, like me, who enjoys a Dungeons & Dragons/Wizards of the Coast -type battle system; then even if you don't follow the guides, they may hopefully at least provide interesting reading and food for thought...
If you're finding the game a bit too complicated, you might want to start by looking at my tips and tricks page, which explains some of the game's fundamental concepts.

Equipment
I equip the following at endgame:
Superior Targeting Computer (this is how T3-M4 has the Feat Weapon Specialization: Blaster Pistol; you can't obtain this Feat simply by leveling up) - Universal Computer Interface - Advanced Computer Tool (these 2 together raise the Computer Use Skill by 10 - list of Droid Upgrades here) - Baragwin Shield Disruptor - (not the most powerful, but has the huge advantage that it can be used an unlimited number of times - Droid Weapons list) - Baragwin Droid Shield (again, conventionally this isn't the most powerful Shield, but it can be used an unlimited number of times - list of Droid Shields here) - Droid Heavy Plating Type 3 (this is the 2nd best Droid Plating available - Droid Armor list) - Mandalorian Heavy Pistol (x2) (I give the best 2 Blaster Pistols to Carth, but these are also rather powerful - Blaster Pistols list)
