KOTOR 2 - Visas Builds
This file details some considerations to make for a Visas build, and may eventually contain a few Visas builds from different playthroughs. We'll see how far I take it.
Visas starts and remains a Sentinel. With her starting stats, she can reasonably be built as either a DEX-based melee combatant, or as a CHA-based caster.
Sentinel Class Skills: Computer Use, Stealth, Awareness, Security, Treat Injury
======================
Notes:
Dialog tree
Obtained on the Hawk. She appears after you get 1/4 way to either Dark or Light
Can give you Force Sight, which lets you see aura (alignment) of NPCs
For male main characters, if her influence is higher than Handmaiden's, then Handmaiden gets mad at you. So if you want to convert/romance Handmaiden, do not talk to Visas until after you do
Influence gained/lost via dialog and actions when she is in your party (but mostly dialog)
Influence locations:
Almost Anywhere
Kill innocents
Ebon Hawk
After initial fight, say you're not going to kill her
After leaving infirmary, say you didn't want to hurt her (I think this gets a Visas gain even though she's unconscious. Unconfirmed.)
When talking later, generally say nice Light-Sidey stuff. Several gains possible.
Dantooine
When you turn in the atmospheric sensors for 3000 credits, and comment on the hidden intelligence.
In cave, tell mercs that Vrook belongs in his cage (also gets Dark Side points)
Nar Shaada
Help out guy being harrassed by thugs. Let him go afterward.
Heal sick refugee
After you return TT-31 to TT-32, extort TT-32 for the plans and get a discount (requires high INT)
Onderon
Side with rabble rouser, support him in calling for an end to the monarchy.
After that, talk to Anda, agree to remove the guard captains, oppose the Queen when Visas asks you about it
When guys arguing politics in Merchant Quarter talk to you, side with Vaklu
Give open starport visa to mother near the entrance, then say "The darkness clouds your mind..." line 
When meeting with Kavar is raided, tell her to kill people. Or say you forbid it for LSP and influence loss.
