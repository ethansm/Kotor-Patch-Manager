Hanharr
 | 
 | 
This enraged wookie outcast from Kashyyyk can now be found on Nar Shaddaa working for the Exchange as a Bounty Hunter. Those who are strongly committed to the Dark Side of the force are the only ones likely to gain his loyalty. Hanharr's main rival, Mira, is constantly getting the upper hand, which tends to make his demeanor that much more unpleasant and competitive... almost to a fault.
If you can manage to tame him, his built-in toughness, damage resistance and battle rage turns him into a melee powerhouse that does not easily fall in combat and can slice through foes faster than many other companions, Jedi or no.
