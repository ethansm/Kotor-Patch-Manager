Rebuild HK-47[edit | edit source]
Aboard the Ebon Hawk, you'll find a damaged HK unit in the Storage Compartment behind the low security door in the northeast wall of the main hold:
 | Damaged HK Unit: [This looks like the remains of an HK unit, but older and more corroded than the one you encountered on Peragus. Its power core still carries a charge, but a number of critical parts appear to be missing.]
 | 2. Step away.
 | Rebuild HK-47: Bonus Mission
You found a damaged HK-unit in one of the cargo holds. It looks like it could be repaired if you found the right parts.
 | 1. [Repair] Diagnose the droid.
 | Damaged HK Unit: [It looks as if this droid is missing four critical components - its droid processor, a replacement droid chassis, the control cluster that's supposed to be stored in the chassis, and its vocabulator.]
 | Rebuild HK-47: Bonus Mission
The damaged HK unit on the Ebon Hawk requires a droid processor, a droid chassis, a control cluster, and a vocabulator. If you bring them to the droid and install them, that should make it operational again.
The parts can be acquired from the remains of HK-50 droids encountered, or bought from merchants:

The most straighforward way to get all four parts is to get the vocabulator on Peragus II and the control cluster on Telos, then go to Nar Shaddaa, where Kodin can sell the droid processor and T3-M4 can get the chassis from the droid warehouse.
However, this means HK-47 cannot be fully repaired and join your party until you escape the destruction of Goto's Yacht, preventing you from using any security console there to upgrade HK-47's combat algorithms (Dexterity: +1), or gaining Influence by getting HK-47 to install cryogenic power cells in the refugee quad's Airspeeder. You can fully repair HK-47 beforehand, though, if you have the vocabulator from Peragus II and the control cluster from Telos, buy the droid processor from 1B-8D on Onderon or get it from Akkere's remains on Dantooine, then keep it in your inventory (do not install it in HK-47) until you've spoken to Kodin on Nar Shaddaa to buy the chassis (you could also buy the droid processor from Kodin first, then buy the chassis from 1B-8D).
HK-47 can also be fully repaired before Goto's Yacht if using Restored Content Mod, by exiting the Ebon Hawk on Telos to another HK-50 encounter, where the player may salvage another component from the destroyed HK-50s before buying the final component from Kodin.
If you miss getting a part from a source to which you cannot return, then you can buy it from Kodin or 1B-8D: for example, if you miss the vocabulator on Peragus II then one of them can sell it to you if a droid processor, chassis and control cluster are in your inventory when you first speak to them.
Once you've escaped Peragus II aboard the Ebon Hawk, you can install the vocabulator from the remains of the HK-50 assassin droid encountered in its administration level upon returning from the dormitories:
 | 1. [Repair] Take the vocabulator and insert it in the core.
 | Damaged HK Unit: [Success] [You have installed the droid's vocabulator.]
 | Rebuild HK-47: Bonus Mission
You have installed the vocabulator in the HK droid on the Ebon Hawk. All that remains is the droid processor, a droid chassis, and a control cluster.
Once you've left Telos aboard the Ebon Hawk, you can install the control cluster from the remains of one of the HK-50 droids encountered on the polar plateau:
 | 1. [Repair] Initialize the control cluster.
 | Damaged HK Unit: [Success] [You have installed the droid's control cluster.]
 | Rebuild HK-47: Bonus Mission
You have installed the control cluster in the HK droid on the Ebon Hawk. All that remains is the droid chassis, and the droid processor.
The processor and chassis can both be acquired on Nar Shaddaa, the former from Kodin in the refugee landing pad, and the latter from the remains of one of the HK-50 droids encountered by T3-M4 in the droid warehouse:
 | 1. [Repair] Install the droid's processor.
 | 1. [Repair] Reinforce the droid's damaged chassis with the one you have.
 | Damaged HK Unit: [Success] [You have installed the droid's processor.]
 | Damaged HK Unit: [Success] [You have installed the droid's chassis. It looks like it will make its chest stop sparking, which is a minor relief.]
 | Rebuild HK-47: Bonus Mission
You have installed the processor in the HK-47 droid on the Ebon Hawk. All that remains is the droid chassis.
 | Rebuild HK-47: Bonus Mission
You have installed the chassis in the HK droid on the Ebon Hawk. All that remains is the droid processor. At least the sparks have stopped.

The HK Protocol Pacifist Package can be bought from Geeda in the refugee landing pad on Nar Shaddaa after resolving the Trade War and stabilizing Dantooine and Onderon. It can also be bought from Kodin on Nar Shaddaa's refugee landing pad or 1B-8D in Onderon's western square if you still have the four parts required to rebuild HK-47 (droid processor, chassis, control cluster and vocabulator) in your inventory when you first speak to them.
