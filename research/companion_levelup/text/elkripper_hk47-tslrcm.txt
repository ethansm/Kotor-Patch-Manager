Shuffle the feat order if you like. I typically don't get HK-47 to level 30, but by that point he's pretty much out of useful feats anyway.
This is a basic Blaster Rifle build. It prioritizes damage output. This build plus a fully upgradable blaster rifle with the best crafted upgrades in the game got me through the TSLRCM HK-50 Droid Factory without much trouble.
HK-47 should take at least 10 Security (15 on Hard) for the Droid Factory, because he will be solo for that.
Alternatively, he could dual-wield blaster pistols and take the Rapid Shot line (for more opportunities to hit), but between Two-Weapon Fighting and not getting the first Rapid Shot feat for free, that'd mean less of the Precise Shot line. This would probably be the best option for steady damage output, but might result in fewer big damange numbers, and big numbers are fun.
The Sniper Shot line plus a Keen weapon (whether pistols or rifle) might also be a reasonable choice for potentially higher but somewhat less reliable damage. As a companion, that'd pair well with party members with Sneak Attack, because Sniper Shot has a chance to stun (not a big chance, so couldn't rely on it, but it'd be fun when it happened).
I left the higher ranks of Toughness and the Close Combat line to the end because if HK-47 is taking significant damage or is close to enemies, then someething went wrong. There's an arguement to be made for taking them earlier, though, because it'd help him get through fights that went poorly. Do whatever you find valuable.
