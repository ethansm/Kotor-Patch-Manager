KOTOR 2 - Handmaiden Unarmed Soldier
This version of Handmaiden is an unarmed specialist who remains a Solider and never becomes a Jedi. In practice, you may want to switch at some point. But you probably want to take Soldier to either 18 or 22 first, to not lose too much from the Unarmed Specialist feat line. Or just keep her a Solider - she's pretty effective even with no Jedi levels.
