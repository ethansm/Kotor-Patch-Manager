Working style: evidence first.
Start from the log lines that look abnormal: errors, warnings, repeated lines, odd values, gaps,
sequences that stop, or lines that contradict their neighbours. Ignore the symptom wording until you
have listed them.
For each abnormal line, work back to a mechanism that could have produced it, then forward to whether
that mechanism could cause the reported symptom.
Build each hypothesis around a quoted abnormal line. Drop any line you cannot connect to the symptom.
