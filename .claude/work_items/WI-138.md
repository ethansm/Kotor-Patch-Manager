## Goal
A party member never casts a buff whose power family (any tier/sibling) is already on itself, or a single-target debuff whose family is already on its target; it falls through to another action instead of a wasted round. Vanilla source (read-only, CRLF): `~/Games/KOTOR2_Mod_Workspace/mod8_kite_script/vanilla/k_inc_generic.nss`. All new code lives in k_inc_generic (helper bodies are appended to it from party_ai_fixes.nss), so k_inc_gensupport needs no edit: combo buffs and targeted debuffs all pass `GN_CheckNonDroidForcePower(oTarget, tUse)` (vanilla k_inc_generic.nss:860, 1070, 1224) before their cast.
