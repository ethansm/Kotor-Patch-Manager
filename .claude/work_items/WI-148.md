## Goal
SortFilterBufferRow (InventorySortFilter.cpp:329) starts a new populate only when `!g_inPop || idx <= g_lastIdx`. If a populate ever ends without reaching H6c (SortFilterFlush), and the next populate's first row that passes the filter has a higher loop index than the last buffered one (CheckFilter skips the low indices), the old rows stay in g_buf. Their item pointers may be freed, and emitBuffered then passes them to CreateItemEntry: a possible crash. Close the gap.
## Steps
1. Add a populate generation that the engine itself changes: the row counter at [ebp+F_Counter] restarts each populate, and the list pointer at ebp+F_List is per call. Record `g_popEbp` and `g_popCounter0 = rd<int>(ebp, F_Counter)` in startPopulate (:286).
2. In SortFilterBufferRow, also restart when `rd<int>(ebp, F_Counter) < g_lastCounter` (counter went backwards: a new list) or `ebp != g_popEbp`. Track `g_lastCounter` per row.
3. Clear the buffer (`g_n = 0; g_inPop = false`) at the panel-instance change in SortFilterTabClick (:306) as well.
4. Keep the existing `stale buffer ... discarded` logf (:330) for every discard path, adding `reason=idx|counter|ebp`.
