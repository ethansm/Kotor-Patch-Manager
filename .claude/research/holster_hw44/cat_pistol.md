## Checked, no finding

- Stagger and slow motion: the dual stagger is scaled by `slowMs(staggerFor(...), slowHolsterX)` at Holster.cpp:2526. The pre-fire block at 2492 calls `slideResolve` without slowX, which is harmless because grabPct is not scaled (HolsterTune.h:750-756). `staggerFor` tests `isTarget`, which is a node-name property (Holster.cpp:2357, 1279), so the stagger applies to stow as well as draw. The 100 ms stall guards stay real time (HolsterSlide.h:136-143 pump, 151-155 touch), as documented.
- Pole bias window: gated at `f < 0.30` (HolsterReach.h:185) with weight going to 0 at 0.30. Applied only to Hip and Thigh (Holster.cpp:1733). Off by default.
- Left-hand mirror: pole x mirrored at HolsterReach.h:157. Carry outward axis `ouL` at Holster.cpp:1707 (used by the bulge, the via offset at 1723, and the hinge at 1715). Grip-pole direction comes from real geometry and needs no mirror.
- Unreachable target (short arm): `solveTwoBone` clamps d to [lo, hi] (HolsterReach.h:75-77) and places the elbow at the clamp, so the solve does not snap. `reach` is only logged (Holster.cpp:1776, 1869). The carried weapon is lerped between hand and mount by k (Holster.cpp about 1925-1950), so there is no pop at landing.
- Topology boundary Thigh / Hip at -0.66: u is 0 on both sides (HolsterReach.h:289, 294), so this one is continuous.
- NaN guards: HolsterReach.h:60-61, 185, 302-304, 644, 659; Holster.cpp:1733 (qfinite). `slowHolsterClamp` maps NaN to 1 (HolsterSlide.h:239-242).
