## Where model, texture, fit are chosen
- Model and texture: not chosen in ShowItem. The engine FnRender (0x008cd2c0, line 41) builds the scene from the item pointer and category handed over via view+0x304 / view+0x2fc.
- Light rig "upgitem_light" (or g_cfg.rig): chosen in H1 (460-467), not in ShowItem. sceneview::addModel is called there, not here.
- Fit values: FitEntry {row, var, flags, c[3], e[3]} from kFit (ItemFitTable.inc:3), looked up by fitLookup(id, v0) for cat == 2 only. flags & 1 = EMPTY (no mesh). Meaning of c and e is in setupPresentation, not read here.
- Category: kItemCategory (ItemModelTable.inc:4). Header comment (line 2): 0 = no preview, 2 = plain model, 4 = armor on male-medium body.
