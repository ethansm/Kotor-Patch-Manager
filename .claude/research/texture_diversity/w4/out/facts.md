## CONFLICTS
1. Republic cog: W3 symbols_1 CORRECTIONS says "the cog is the Senate seal". Canon Republic crest page (republic_1.txt:245) calls the 8-spoke sigil "Republic cog". Canon page wins. The seal is a separate emblem.
2. Old Sith hex: W3 sith_jedi_1 CORRECTIONS says "shape not stated". Canon Sith Eternal crest (symbols_3.txt:292) says dagger. Dagger wins. W1 A8 "refuted" is right on shape.
3. Mythosaur: W1 A13 says no source calls it Mandalore's symbol. Legends crest page (symbols_5.txt:346) says "a traditional symbol1] of the Mand'alor" (citation mark in source). Legends quote wins for that claim. Canon kyr'bes (symbols_5.txt:315) sets the design.
4. Republic Navy nine-spoke: W1 A1 says it appears only in an unread snippet. Fetched chunk has it verbatim (republic_1.txt:152). W3 wins.
5. Sith red plates: W1 A8-01 says avoid red (Sith Eternal gammaplast; source page not in W3). W3 sith_jedi_1.txt:341 says commandos wore red. Both usable: red = commandos, silver = troopers.
