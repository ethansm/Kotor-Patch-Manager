# sym_01 emblem sheet review (S01-S12)

- era: OTHER (lead: High Republic appearances, no 3956 BBY date)
- shows: usable_emblem

### S02 First Order insignia

## TOP3
- S09: two-tone badge with chevron barbs; reads at 128 px and suits a mercenary or Exchange crew patch on cloth.
- S11: repeated tally ticks; cheapest paint for gang or crew marks on a commoner jacket.
- S02: sixteen-spoke ring in black on grey; strongest silhouette and contrast at 128 px.
