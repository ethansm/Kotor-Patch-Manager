# A7 Mandalorians and mercenaries (wave 1 recon)
Corrections to the brief:
- Jaig eyes is a Mando'a combat honour painted as helmet markings (Rex, later Kanan's eye mask), not an eye lens and not a glow.
- Cannok is a non-sentient creature on Dxun and Telos IV, not a mercenary.
- No fetched KOTOR II source gives mercenary or Sith-hired armour colours; Telos mercenary camps are named only.
## A7-01: Battle Circle blue armour recolour
- who: Mandalorian Recruit, Battle Circle warrior (Dxun)
- type: COLOR
- zone: chest_center, upper_arm, shoulder_L, shoulder_R
- prompt: Matte steel-blue recolour over chest and upper arms, with darker navy edge shading on plate seams. Flat paint, no gloss.
- palette: steel blue, navy
- lore: VERIFIED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Davrel ; https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Tagren (Legends-tagged; KOTOR II listed as first appearance)
- note: Blue is what both named Dxun Battle Circle fighters wear and reads at distance. Neo-Crusader rank-colour claim is forum-only, not used.
