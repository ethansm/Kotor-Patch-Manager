# A8 Force users and cults: add-on concepts (Wave 1)

Claim check: Gemini "six-pointed hex Old Sith emblem" is REFUTED (see A8-04).

## A8-01: Silver plate bands (Sith Trooper)
- who: Sith Trooper, Elite Trooper (Sith Empire)
- type: TRIM
- zone: chest_center, shoulder_L, shoulder_R
- prompt: Bright silver metal plate bands across the chest and shoulders, riveted edges, dark gaps between plates, flat painted metallic sheen.
- palette: silver, charcoal, black
- lore: VERIFIED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/sith_empire_uniforms (Legends-tagged; "sixteen silver plates", reflective finish)
- note: Large bands read at distance. Avoid red trooper plates: the red gammaplast on https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/sith_trooper_armor is Sith Eternal (TROS), not KOTOR-era.
## A8-02: Crimson officer sash
- who: Sith Officer of higher rank (Legends Fleet Commander style); Sith Lieutenant if the in-game model matches
- type: TRIM
- zone: shoulder_R, chest_center
- prompt: Diagonal crimson sash running from right shoulder down across the chest, narrow gold edging on both sides.
- palette: crimson, gold, black
- lore: VERIFIED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/sith_empire_uniforms (Fleet Commanders wore a red sash from the right shoulder)
- note: One bold diagonal band reads at camera distance and separates officers from troopers.
## A8-03: Officer rank tabs and collar marks
- who: Sith Officer, Sith Lieutenant
- type: EMBLEM
- zone: shoulder_L, shoulder_R, collar
- prompt: Small dark-red rank tabs with two gold bars on each shoulder; tiny red and gold marks at both collar points.
- palette: dark red, gold, black
- lore: VERIFIED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/sith_empire_uniforms (rank insignia on both shoulders; red and gold command symbols on the collar)
- note: Shoulder tabs are big enough to read; collar marks are small, so keep them to two simple shapes.
## A8-04: Dagger hex charm (Sith assassin)
- who: Sith Assassin, Sith cultist (self-decoration)
- type: EMBLEM
- zone: chest_center
- prompt: Small flat dagger-shaped emblem painted in black with a red inlay, centred on the upper chest.
- palette: black, red, dark grey
- lore: INFERRED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/sith_assassin (hex charm "typically linked to Sith assassins"; era unlabelled); https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/sith_eternal_crest (dagger-shaped, Sith Eternal, first appearance TROS)
- note: CLAIM CHECK: Gemini's "six-pointed hex Old Sith emblem" is REFUTED. Sources say dagger-shaped with no point count, Sith Eternal era, no Old Sith origin. Paint a dagger, not six points.
## A8-05: Dark side forearm tattoo
- who: Sith Acolyte, dark side cultist with bare forearms
- type: TATTOO
- zone: upper_arm, forearm
- prompt: Black and dark-red angular tribal bands wrapping the forearm and upper arm on bare skin, sharp spiral lines.
- palette: black, dark red
- lore: INFERRED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/sith_tattoo (body art of dark side adherents, applied with Sith pincerbugs; design, placement and colour INVENTED)
- note: Only for bare-arm atlas areas. The source gives no design, so the pattern is INVENTED.
## A8-06: Scorched hem (dark side wear)
- who: Dark Jedi Apprentice, Sith Academy student (Korriban)
- type: WEAR
- zone: hem, shin
- prompt: Scorched black burn patches and soot-grey frayed edges along the hem, scattered small burn holes, charcoal smudges.
- palette: charcoal, black, ash grey
- lore: INVENTED | no source found for dark side clothing damage
- note: Flat wear only, no geometry needed; dark low-contrast marks read at distance.
## A8-07: Light-grey Jedi robe recolour (Handmaiden)
- who: Handmaiden (Brianna) wearing her mother's Jedi robe
- type: COLOR
- zone: whole_garment
- prompt: Recolour the whole garment soft light grey with slight cloth wear, and a faint cream border along the hem.
- palette: light grey, cream
- lore: VERIFIED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/brianna (mother's robe "light-grey"; later "heavy set of grayed robes" in Jedi Master style)
- note: Whole-garment recolour is the safest large-area change; grey stands out against the white Echani base.
