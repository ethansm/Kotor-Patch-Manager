# A5 Nar Shaddaa civilian and nightlife add-on concepts (wave 1)
Scope: refugees, bar patrons, dancers, smugglers, swoop riders, gamblers, cargo crews. Setting ~3956 BBY.
## A5-01: Refugee numbered sleeve tag
- who: Refugee (planet 302, census Refugee:11)
- type: EMBLEM
- zone: shoulder_L, upper_arm
- prompt: A small rectangular cloth tag stitched on the upper arm, with a black serial number printed on off-white. Plain stitching, no logo.
- palette: off-white, black, rust-brown
- lore: INFERRED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Refugee_Sector (sector held "millions of refugees"; the page describes no tag or ID)
- note: Bright rectangle on the shoulder reads at distance; the ID-tag idea is extrapolated, not sourced.

- who: Refugee; Refugee Sector residents
- type: WEAR
- zone: hem, knee
- prompt: Coarse dark thread stitching a torn hem closed in visible patches. Mismatched cloth patches, several stitches, frayed edge. Thread colour: dark brown.
- palette: dark brown thread, grey-brown patch, faded tan
- lore: INFERRED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Refugee_Sector ("the sector's conditions were appalling"; residents "continued to live in poverty")
