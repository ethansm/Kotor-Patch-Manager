# A1 Republic and TSF add-on concepts (wave 1)
## GEMINI CLAIM CHECK: "Bendu 8-spoked cog" = PARTIAL
- Right: eight spokes converge on a central disk; the page calls it the "Republic cog" and traces it to Bendu monks' numerology (republic_crest page).
- Unsupported: no source ties the cog to TSF; a "nine-spoke Navy variant" appears only in an unread search snippet; a Legends snippet says "eight-rayed star inside a circle with dashed edges" (conflicts with "cog").
## A1-01: Republic cog chest emblem
- who: Republic Officer, Republic soldier, Republic Navy
- type: EMBLEM
- zone: chest_left
- prompt: Small white eight-spoked cog emblem on a dark navy disc, spokes converging on a central ring. Flat, crisp edges, no gradient, no text.
- palette: white, navy
- lore: INFERRED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/republic_crest
- note: Design VERIFIED on the page, uniform placement INFERRED; valid for 3956 BBY as the Republic's own emblem; single high-contrast shape reads at distance.
## A1-02: TSF shoulder shield patch
- who: TSF Officer, Telos Security Force enforcer
- type: EMBLEM
- zone: shoulder_L
- prompt: Dark blue shield-shaped shoulder patch with one wide gold horizontal bar across its middle. Flat, sharp outline, no text, no shading.
- palette: dark blue, gold
- lore: INVENTED | no source (TSF page names Lt. Dol Grenn, Lt. Yima, Officer Soka Linu, Officer Batu Rem; only a caption "A TSF officer in full uniform")
- note: Deliberately a different shape from the cog so TSF and Republic read apart at distance; not canon.
