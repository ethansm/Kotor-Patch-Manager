# A3 Czerka concept cards (wave 1)
Logo check: the "sharp-angular geometric" claim is UNVERIFIED. No readable source describes the shape. Wookieepedia, the KOTOR wiki and the Villains logo file page all returned HTTP 402. Prompts below keep the logo shape neutral.
## A3-01: Czerka chest emblem
- who: Czerka Security, Czerka Office Staff
- type: EMBLEM
- zone: chest_left
- prompt: Small simple corporate badge, a plain abstract mark in off-white outlined on a dark olive square. Flat print, no text, crisp edges.
- palette: olive green, off-white, charcoal
- lore: INFERRED | https://deadlystream.com/topic/8318-modczerka-armor-and-appearance-fix/ (Light Armor "bearing the Czerka colors and logo"; this is a KOTOR 1 armour mod, shape and colour not given)
- note: One large shape reads at distance. Shape and colour must be checked against an in-game screenshot before painting.
## A3-02: Paramilitary rank bars
- who: Czerka Security (paramilitary)
- type: TRIM
- zone: chest_center, upper_arm
- prompt: Two to four short horizontal bars stacked on the chest, pale grey on dark green fabric. Bars show rank. Plain flat stripes, no text.
- palette: pale grey, dark green
- lore: INFERRED | https://deadlystream.com/topic/8318-modczerka-armor-and-appearance-fix/ (rank shown by stat bars on chest for men, sleeves for women; KOTOR 1 armour mod, bar colour not given)
- note: Three or four bars are readable at distance. Bar colour is a design guess.
## A3-03: Commander green recolour
- who: Czerka Commander, Czerka Supervisor (non-paramilitary)
- type: COLOR
- zone: whole_garment
- prompt: Recolor the whole garment a deep forest green with slightly darker shading along the seams. Keep fabric folds. No logos or text.
- palette: deep forest green, peach orange
- lore: INFERRED | https://deadlystream.com/topic/9142-modrevised-czerka-armor-and-appearance-fix/ ("all-green Czerka commander's uniform"; v1.4 "darker greens and a more peach shade of orange", a modder's approximation of KOTOR 1 skins)
- note: Recolour only, no geometry. Whole-body colour reads well at distance. Confirm the KOTOR 2 Telos colour in game first.
## A3-04: Office staff collar and cuff piping
- who: Czerka Office Staff (human, Twi'lek, Rodian, Ithorian)
- type: TRIM
- zone: collar, cuff
- prompt: Thin single-colour piping along the collar and both sleeve cuffs, in light grey. Clean office-wear finish, no pattern, no text.
- palette: light grey, slate
- lore: INFERRED | https://deadlystream.com/topic/9142-modrevised-czerka-armor-and-appearance-fix/ (non-paramilitary staff wear Czerka uniforms, Rodian and Ithorian variants; the piping itself is INVENTED)
- note: Piping under 2 px on the 1024 atlas will vanish at camera distance. Paint at least 3 px wide.
## A3-05: Security Division shoulder tab
- who: Czerka Security Division guard
- type: EMBLEM
- zone: shoulder_L
- prompt: Small rectangular shoulder tab on the left shoulder, black with a short white bar and a single white dot. Flat print, plain, no words.
- palette: black, white
- lore: INVENTED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/atallo (unit exists: Atallo "was assassinated by members of the Czerka Security Division"; the markings are invented)
- note: Black and white is high contrast at distance. No source for any real Czerka security marking.
## A3-06: Mercenary belt pouch row
- who: Czerka Mercenary (hired guard)
- type: HARDWARE
- zone: belt
- prompt: Row of four flat-painted ammo pouches across the belt, dark grey with brass snap buttons. Painted flat, no straps or hanging parts.
- palette: dark grey, brass
- lore: INVENTED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Jana_Lorso (mercenaries Lorso hired to guard her office exist; their dress is not described, so the pouches are invented)
- note: The pouch row reads as a belt silhouette. Drawn flat, so no geometry is needed.
## A3-07: Restoration Zone soot and scorch
- who: Czerka Guard, Czerka Mercenary (Telos Restoration Zone)
- type: WEAR
- zone: thigh, hip_pocket
- prompt: Soot-black scorch marks and smudged grime across the thigh and hip, heavier on one side. Dark smears only, no sharp edges, no text.
- palette: soot black, ash grey
- lore: INFERRED | https://www.gry-online.pl/poradniki/star-wars-knights-of-the-old-republic-ii-the-sith-lords/telos-planetside-telos-restoration-zone/zc3216 (armed fights and mercenary caches in the zone; the guide gives no clothing detail)
- note: Wear is the cheapest read at distance. Works on any Telos outfit without geometry.
## A3-08: Peragus fuel stain on hem
- who: Peragus Miner, Mining Facility Worker
- type: STAIN
- zone: hem, knee
- prompt: Dark fuel stains and oily streaks across the lower hem and knee, pooling at the bottom edge. Matte black-brown, soaked look, no text.
- palette: black-brown, dull amber
- lore: INFERRED | https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Peragus_II (fuel was the facility's main export; miners used sonic detonators and ion charges). The fetched text does NOT name Czerka as owner, so do not label this card Czerka.
- note: A fuel stain on the hem reads without geometry. Keep it off the Czerka label until the owner is confirmed.
## A3-09: Staff name badge on hip
- who: Czerka staff at Peragus II or Telos (miners, officers)
- type: HARDWARE
- zone: hip_pocket
- prompt: Flat rectangular name badge clipped to the hip pocket, white plastic look with a blue top bar and a short dark text line. Printed, no clip hanging.
- palette: white, blue, dark grey
- lore: INFERRED | UNFETCHED (search snippet: Peragus staff appear in holorecords and as corpses "which bears his name", citing starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Coorta; that page was a placeholder when read)
- note: Name text is illegible at distance; the blue bar is the readable part. Badge design is INVENTED.
## A3-10: Department code stencil on back
- who: Czerka workers (mining, office, security)
- type: PATTERN
- zone: upper_back
- prompt: Large black stencil code across the upper back, blocky military-style letters and digits, slightly faded. Two short lines, no other decoration.
- palette: stencil black, faded grey
- lore: INVENTED | no source found for Czerka department codes
- note: Stencil text is unreadable at game distance. Paint it as a block shape, not as real words or numbers.
