## BEYOND
1. 2026 tool state (primary READMEs unless noted): RVC needs CUDA 12.8 torch builds for RTX 50; a future RVCv3 base is promised to need less data. Applio: MIT; README gives no dataset minutes (third-party guides say 10-30 min, unverified). Seed-VC: GPL-3.0, archived read-only since 2025-11-21, zero-shot from 1-30 s reference. OpenVoice V2: MIT, zero-shot (V1 confirmed; V2 inheritance not stated). kNN-VC: training-free, ~5 min reference, 16 kHz, HiFi-GAN vocoder needs training. GPT-SoVITS: MIT, 5 s zero-shot. Sources: github.com/IAHispano/Applio, github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc, github.com/myshell-ai/OpenVoice, github.com/bshall/knn-vc, github.com/RVC-Boss/GPT-SoVITS
2. Small-data route: kNN-VC is the only training-free option found. Its README says gains "diminish beyond 5 minutes" and gives no lower bound. Seed-VC needs no training but is archived, so vendor a pinned copy under GPL-3.0 if used.
3. Alien vs sound-alike: ffmpeg 7.0.2 rubberband defaults to `formant=shifted` (checked with `ffmpeg -h filter=rubberband`), so a plain `pitch=` also moves formants (monster effect). Sound-alike needs `rubberband=pitch=X:formant=preserved`. aeval with `t`, acrusher, tremolo, aecho and alimiter all run in the local build.
4. K2 StreamVoice hygiene: headers are malformed (RIFF size 50, data size 0, empty `fact`, declared 8-bit). Some files are byte-identical under different names: `rakahapm2.wav` = `rakata2.wav` (md5 80c349c9...). Dedupe by hash and rebuild headers before any pipeline.
5. Legal framing (secondary sources, not official text): NO FAKES Act S.4591 / H.R.8915 reintroduced May 2026, cleared Senate Judiciary, no floor vote by June 2026. TN ELVIS Act covers voice since 2024-07-01. CA AB 2602 voids AI-replica contract clauses unless represented. Private test on your own game copy with no redistribution is low exposure. Publishing clones of identifiable real actors is the risk. Pitch-shift "sound-alikes" are not a privacy shield (C4). EU AI Act Art. 50 date unverified.
