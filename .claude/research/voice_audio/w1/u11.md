C2: PARTIAL - Whisper gives words, not phonemes: openai/whisper page says MIT, no phoneme output, no word_timestamps mention (https://github.com/openai/whisper). Phoneme route = forced alignment: MFA is MIT, Kaldi-based (https://github.com/MontrealCorpusTools/Montreal-Forced-Aligner), but the page does not confirm phone-level TextGrid output. wav2vec2 phoneme CTC not checked. Audio2Face is NVIDIA Open Model License, not a .lip source.
C3: CONFIRMED (file layout) - scratch round-trip PASS: `tmp/u11/lipcheck.py` builds and re-parses a 7-key .lip; `tmp/u11/rhubarb_to_lip.py` (~35 lines) converts a Rhubarb TSV + WAV to .lip and re-parses with no rule violations. In-game validity UNSURE (not tested in game).

Layout (verified on 677 real K2 files, `Override/*.lip`): 8-byte "LIP V1.0", f32 length @0x08, u32 count @0x0C, 5-byte keys (f32 time, u8 shape) from 0x10. Rules hold on all 677: first key = 0.0, last key = length (within 1e-5). Shape bytes 0-15 all used. Keys 2-207, length 0.55-17.8 s.

## Rhubarb -> KOTOR map (proposed; names = rakata convention, see BEYOND 2)
X->0 (neutral), A->11 (m,p,b), B->12 (t,d; generic consonant, coarse), C->2 (eh), D->3 (ah), E->4 (oh), F->5 (ooh), G->8 (f,v), H->14 (l). Unused by Rhubarb: 1 ee, 6 y, 7 s/t, 9 ng, 10 th, 13 sh, 15 k/g. Lossy.
Pipeline: `rhubarb -o cues.tsv line.wav` -> `python3 -I rhubarb_to_lip.py cues.tsv line.wav out.lip` (Rhubarb reads WAV/OGG only, per its README).
