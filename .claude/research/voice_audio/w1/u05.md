# u05: alien VO reuse, K1 vs K2 voice audio (2026-10-13)

Method: md5 of 33,923 audio files (K2 StreamVoice 18,322; K2 StreamSounds 832; K1 streamwaves 13,799; K1 streamsounds 970). DLG VO_ResRefs parsed with stdlib GFF code from K2 modules/*.mod|rim|erf (type 2029, 1,295 DLG resrefs) plus chitin.key (30 GBL DLGs). Scripts and hashes: `.claude/waves/w10-voice/tmp/u05/`.

C1: PARTIAL - Hutt and Shyriiwook DLG lines use only AVO_ clips (no per-line VO), but AVO folders hold 3-9 clips (not 8-15), and AVO is 2.2% of 17,104 DLG VO lines; named VO is 99.0% unique audio. Evidence: `tmp/u05/analyze_mod_vo.py` output.
C2: CONFIRMED - 13,781 of 13,799 K1 streamwaves WAVs have no byte-identical K2 StreamVoice match; 0 base-name overlap; K1 n_g* generic alien clips (421) match no K2 AVO clip by bytes.
