Method: K2 nwscript = `/home/ethansm/Games/KOTOR2_Mod_Workspace/mod8_kite_script/vanilla/nwscript.nss` (6321 lines, md5 b487ad80, same as script_recompile vanilla). C1: PARTIAL - In handmaiden.DLG, VO_ResRef (CResRef, all 1064 nodes, max len 16, e.g. gblhandmaiden288) and CameraID (INT, only 5 nodes, CameraAngle 6) are real. Delay is DWORD, 0xFFFFFFFF on 1058/1064 nodes. Label is "AnimList" (not Animlist), a List of Participant + Animation WORD. "-1 waits for audio" is not what the sources say: rakata says 0xFFFFFFFF on a node with a sound name takes root DelayEntry and sets a WaitFlags bit (https://synchro.codeberg.page/rakata/api/rakata_generics/dlg/struct.DlgNode.html, "derived, not attested"). Units: seconds (same page; xoreos dlgfile.h "number of seconds").

C2: REFUTED - "1-node DLG with NoZoomIn=1": 0 of 1016 K2 .dlg files under KOTOR2_Mod_Workspace contain "NoZoomIn" or "PreventZoom" (rg -a -i). xoreos models a root-level bool `_noZoomIn` (https://xoreos.org/doxygen/db/d8e/dlgfile_8h_source.html), not per node. BarkString(obj, strref) to a TLK row is real (nwscript.nss:5116), but the TLK-to-sound link is UNSURE (see OPEN).

C3: PARTIAL - ActionStartConversation(oObj, sDlg, ...) runs the named DLG; blank sDlg = the creature's own DLG (nwscript.nss:3086-3100). Its nConversationType is documented as having "no meaning anymore"; presentation comes from DLG root ConversationType (0 cinematic, 1 computer; handmaiden root = 0) and AnimatedCut. Vanilla: a_gbl_react.nss:52 starts "000react" from an invisible object; k_inc_utility.nss:1952 passes CINEMATIC + bIgnoreStartRange.

C4: CONFIRMED - nwscript.nss: PlaySound L2308 (fn 46, sSoundName only), ActionSpeakString L2276 (fn 39), ActionSpeakStringByStrRef L3279 (fn 240), SpeakOneLinerConversation L4072 (fn 417, sDialogResRef + oTokenTarget). Missing from Gemini: SpeakString 221 (L3176), BarkString 671 (L5116), ActionBarkString 700 (L5244), SoundObjectPlay 413 (L4055), OBJECT_TYPE_SOUND=512 (L88), GetStrRefSoundDuration 571 (L4775).
