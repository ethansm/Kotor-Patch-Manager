## Tools (current state)
- ExtracTOR (UltimaKaosXIII): Windows app, needs .NET 8, read-only, copies out uncompressed; hash list from Jedipedia. https://github-wiki-see.page/m/SWTOR-Slicers/WikiPedia/wiki/Installing-ExtracTOR-and-extracting-the-game-assets
- EasyMYP: not compatible with 64-bit client from Update 7.2.1; "won't be developed any further". Superseded by ExtracTOR.
- Jedipedia File Reader: preview/export one file at a time; replaces EasyMYP/NodeViewer/PugTools.
- Decoders: vgmstream "only decodes"; its FORMATS.md lists `.wem .bnk` with Vorbis, Opus, PTADPCM. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vgmstream/vgmstream/master/doc/FORMATS.md ; ww2ogg (hcs64, BSD-3) handles Wwise RIFF Vorbis only, errors on other codecs. https://github.com/hcs64/ww2ogg

## BEYOND
1. Voice filename encodes context: `cnv_location_<category>_<...>_c2n2_c2n2_98_m.wem` (conversation node, line no., gender). Speaker mapping needs the TORCommunity DB, not the filename. https://github.com/skolia/swtor_restrainingbolt
2. Sizes: full extraction with audio ~100 GB; EasyMYP crashed on `swtor_main_bnk_audio_1.tor` at 16 GB RAM; Slicers "Sound" preset (music+SFX) ~5 GB. https://github-wiki-see.page/m/SWTOR-Slicers/WikiPedia/wiki/Extracting-SWTOR-assets-with-EasyMYP
3. Licence: EA User Agreement (updated 14 May 2026) bars copying/modifying "EA Service, Content"; reverse engineering limited to "source code"; "modified" EA PC game files listed as enforcement data. SWTOR's own EULA not found (swtor.com ToS URL is a marketing page). https://www.ea.com/legal/user-agreement
4. Precedents, not permission: restrainingbolt (Unlicense) edits en-us conversation archives in place with backups; XCOM 2 alien pack credits EA/BioWare and Disney. No DeadlyStream thread covers audio (joshjonasone 2020 tutorial = models only).
5. Usefulness (my judgement): generic Basic barks (soldier, smuggler, thug, bounty hunter, officer) = MEDIUM-LOW, since lines are recorded per SWTOR plot and corpus share of generic barks is unknown. Alien vocalisations = LOW-MEDIUM (shared gibberish pools, few species confirmed).
