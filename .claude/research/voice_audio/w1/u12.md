# u12: AI-voice mod policy and KOTOR voice prior art (2026-10-13)

C1: PARTIAL - Nexus allows AI voices/dialogue only with the "AI-Generated Content" tag (help.nexusmods.com/article/28, "Last updated on September 29, 2026"). No Nexus text bans cloning unconsented actors or limits AI to "non-actor" voices. DeadlyStream forum rules (deadlystream.com/topic/7454) never mention AI; Rule 21 = moderator discretion.
C2: REFUTED - Nexus requires the tag, not a description: "Failure to tag mods in correct fashion will result in moderation" (help.nexusmods.com/article/28). DeadlyStream has no disclosure rule, so nothing supports C2 there.

## Legal context
- SAG-AFTRA 2025 Interactive Media Agreement: ratified Jul 2025 (95.04%); informed consent before digital replica use; binds signatory studios, not modders. Clause text from law-firm summaries; sagaftra.org returned 403.
- NO FAKES Act (S.4591 per search, number unconfirmed): Senate Judiciary approved unanimously Jun 18, 2026 (blackburn.senate.gov); platform liability once known unauthorized; no floor vote found.
- California AB 1836 and AB 2602: effective Jan 1, 2025; contract/deceased-performer rules; no source applies them to hobby mods.
- Lucasfilm: tolerates fan works, does not endorse; no current written fan-mod policy found.
