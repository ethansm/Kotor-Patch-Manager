# w10-voice b11: LORE-ACCURATE EVENT SEEDS, 3951 BBY (4 area packs)

Lens: event seeds grounded in Wookieepedia (Legends) + K2 plot order. Beats only, no final lines.
Tags: `Basic` = Galactic Basic, no audio risk. `AVO <sp> +sub (<Lang>)` = alien clip pool from catalog/k2_avo_bank.tsv with on-screen subtitle.
Phase: `PRE` = before Meetra Surik's arrival (Peragus escape, Telos arrival). `PC` = after her contact. `LATE` = after the Telos IV battle.

## Canon frame (read before writing any line)

- Source status: every Wookieepedia page used is tagged Legends (`{{Top|fa|leg}}` on Citadel Station). K2 itself is Legends. "Canon" here means internal consistency with K2 and the Legends timeline.
- Module numbers: wiki names (Residential Module 082, Entertainment Module 081, Dock Module 126) are in-universe labels, not game codes. Game codes below come from `catalog/gemini_module_claims.tsv` (corrected map).
- Pre-PC pressure is real but not a war. Onderon martial law comes before the civil war. The Exchange/Czerka squeeze on Telos comes before the PC. The fuel crisis does not.
- Wiki title "The Exchange" is a different Neftali business (Wretched Hives). Do not cite it for the crime syndicate.

### Timeline anchors (dates from wiki)
- 3960 BBY: Karath bombards Telos. 3956 BBY: Malak killed at end of Jedi Civil War; Khoonda government starts; Enclave on Dantooine destroyed.
- 3955 BBY: Republic starts Telos Restoration Project under Ithorian herd (Chodo Habat).
- 3952 BBY: Katarr conclave massacre (past by 3951; rumour material).
- 3951 BBY (K2): Peragus escape, Citadel arrival, Nar Shaddaa, Dxun, Iziz, Dantooine, Onderon civil war, Telos IV battle (late 3951), Malachor V end-game.

### Traps (do not violate)
1. "Sith purge" is not an in-universe term. Use **First Jedi Purge** (Sith hunting stray Jedi, post-3956). Do not use "Great Jedi Purge" (Order 66 era).
2. Nihilus's attack on Telos IV is LATE 3951. No Sith fleet, Ravager or sith trooper squads over Telos in PRE/PC scenes.
3. The Telos fuel crisis follows the Peragus Mining Facility destruction (PC event). Do not seed station-wide fuel shortage in PRE.
4. Malak is dead (3956). The active Sith are the Sith Triumvirate. Traya's identity as Kreia is hidden until K2's end. Do not use "Darth Traya", "Darth Nihilus" or "Darth Sion" in public PRE rumours. Verified only as PC-era names.
5. Dantooine: the Jedi Enclave was not rebuilt until after 3951. In PRE there is only ruin and a laigrek-infested sublevel. No active Jedi enclave.
6. Telosian Jedi Academy (Atris, Handmaiden Sisters) is secret. "Telosian Civil War" is thousands of years later. Do not use it.
7. Jekk'Jekk Tarr cyanogen fumes are lethal to Humans and Nikto (wiki). No human or Nikto patrons. Bar serves "nutrient chemicals", not drinks. Droids malfunction inside it.
8. Visquis is alive until PC contact. Saquesh's clearing of the Refugee Sector is PRE. G0-T0 is a droid, head of the Exchange, and lives on a yacht in orbit.
9. Onderon: PRE is martial law and secession talk (General Vaklu vs cousin Queen Talia). The civil war starts after Dantooine (K2 order). Do not place Sith on Onderon in PRE.
10. Clone-era battle droids were stationed in Iziz (Clone Wars). No source found for 3951 droid garrisons. Do not place them without a source.
11. Ithorians speak Basic through a translator collar (wiki). Ithorian dialogue can be Basic with Ithorese flavour. Ithorian AVO exists (39 clips, 239 s) for ambience only.
12. Kel Dor have no AVO pool in catalog. Kel Dor dialogue is Basic only.
