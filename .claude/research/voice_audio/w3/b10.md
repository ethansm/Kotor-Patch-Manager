## 8 parameter hypotheses (all untested in KOTOR II)
1. Per-speaker cooldown 90 s (test 60/120/180).
2. Global ambient gap >= 25 s per area (typical 45 s).
3. Pool minimum 10 DISTINCT audio clips per cue (hash-unique; Jawa 745jawa001-004 identical), else text-only or disabled.
4. No-repeat window: clip can't replay within last max(3, pool/2) plays.
5. Trigger probability p=0.10 per 10 s per eligible NPC (0.03 for small talk).
6. Proximity: trigger inside 384 units with LOS; never beyond 1000 (AIActionPlaySound radius, client handler untraced).
7. Priority: no ambient line within 3 s of scripted/player line; abort on attack.
8. Pity timer for random encounters: base 1 per 12 min per area, +10%/min after 15 min, reset on fire.
