## Ideas
### 1. Translator subtitle on an alien clip (Gran precedent)
- Player: hears an alien line, reads a Basic subtitle.
- Mechanism: DLG node VO_ResRef = bare AVO resref, Text = new TLK strref (v05 F10). Vanilla does this for AVO_GranCmntS1 = TLK 133976.
- Assets: AVO clips on disk; TLK append via `scripts/append_ranged_kite_tlk.py` precedent.
- Feasibility 4 | value 3 | risk 2 | ready now. First test: E7 = one DLG node, AVO clip + appended TLK row.

### 2. Overheard alien exchange, positional
- Player: two aliens talk across a room, optional caption.
- Mechanism: AIActionPlaySound sends the resref to objects within 1000 units (W2). SpeakString floating text unconfirmed.
- Placement: 207TEL cantina (Bith band, Twi'lek musician); 303NAR docks (c_hutt001, c_ithorian001).
- Feasibility 2 | value 4 | risk 2 | needs client-handler RE and a native trigger (not NSS). First test: E1, then E4.

### 3. Table-driven alien barks via alienvo.2da
- Player: barks that follow the engine's own length and emotion cells.
- Mechanism: `Load2DArrays_AlienVO` (0x6ED4D0). Cells: `angry_long` ... `laughter_short`, plus `silence`. Vanilla resolves Shyriiwook 0/60, Selkath 7/46, Jawa 0/6 cells (v05 F5). Repointing empty cells fills the silence.
- Constraint: Override/alienvo.2da is already non-vanilla (md5 61b17261, v05 F6). Merge needed.
- Feasibility 3 | value 4 | risk 2 | needs RE of row selector (O1). First test: E8 = repoint one Shyriiwook cell to existing files.

### 4. K1 Selkath and Wookiee imports
- Player: a full Selkath or Wookiee pool, so repetition is less obvious.
- Mechanism: K1 streamwaves use the same container as K2 VO (58-byte header, MP3 32 kHz 48 kbps). No transcode (v04 F7). Names carry emotion and L/M/S tier, so they map onto the cells.
- Assets: K1 `N_GSEL*`, `N_GWOOK*` (personal use only). `k1_port` has no audio model; use `adopt --kind other`. Gate does not check audio (v04 F16-F20).
- Feasibility 4 after E2 | value 4 | risk 4. First test: E2 with one K1 Wookiee file under StreamVoice.

### 5. Pitch and formant variants of scarce pools
- Player: more variants of Jawa, Wookiee or Selkath clips.
- Mechanism: pitch +/-5-8% with formant preserved (W1 fact). Same voiceprint, so only a small gain.
- Feasibility 5 | value 2 | risk 3 | ready now; `rubberband` in ffmpeg unverified.

## RANKING
1. Idea 1: cheapest; matches vanilla; one TLK append and one DLG test.
2. Idea 4: largest gain (Selkath 7 to 59 clips, Wookiee 4 to 72); blocked on E2 and the licence gate.
3. Idea 3: engine-native, no script; blocked on O1 and the Override merge.
