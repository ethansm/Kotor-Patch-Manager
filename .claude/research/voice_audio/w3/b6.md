## Design
- Offline Python compiler (pattern: gen_r10_tags.py) turns TOML rules into fixed records: speaker class, <=4 atoms (slot,op,value), specificity = atom count, weight, respeak s, speakonce, odds, line ids; each line = audio resref + TLK strref or text.
- Per trigger: fill slots, evaluate rules bucketed by trigger, take highest specificity passing cooldowns, skip recency ring (last 3 per speaker, last 8 global), weighted-random among ties, roll odds.
- Triggers: area enter, combat start/end, party change, saber draw, alignment threshold, flag set, + 6 s poll of <=2 ambient NPCs in radius.
- Pool limit: Selkath AVO <70 s => ~10 lines; Twi'lek/Rodian >1,400 s.
- Examples (illustrative): chest_is_robe=1 + Sith-faction speaker => hostile line; nearby_dead>0 + civilian => fear line once per area; area_visits>=3 => "you again".
