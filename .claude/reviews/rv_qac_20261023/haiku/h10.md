# H10 findings

## H10-F1 [med] edge-case
- Where: patch_manager_mods/companion_selfheal_src/CompanionSelfHeal.cpp:402-407 (dedupe); patch_manager_mods/_shared_src/ActQueue.h:52-56 (useQueued, past window); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:1067-1090 (serverCast, past window); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHudLogic.h:1146-1147 (castStarvedAt comment, past window)
- Claim: selfheal's dedupe refuses only when a round node carries an item id. A queue-hud QL20 server-route Force power already queued on the same companion is invisible to it, so selfheal's item use is not refused.
- Scenario: companion 1 is following (0x3D at head). queue-hud sends its buffered Force heal via serverCast (0x3F plus a spell round node with no itemId). Companion 1 drops under selfheal's hpPct. shouldAutoUse (_shared_src/HealItems.h:65-71, past window) passes because selfheal has no pending, useNow's dedupe reads useQueued=false, and route A appends a second heal behind the spell. Both fire: double heal and a wasted potion.
- Fix: Refuse on any pending round node with a 0x3F in the queue (spell or item), the same roundPending rule castStarvedAt already uses, instead of an itemId-only test.
- Confidence: med (the spell round node carrying no itemId is implied by the QueueHudLogic.h:1146-1147 comment, which says actq::starved is item-only, but I did not check the engine node layout).

## H10-F2 [med] bug
- Where: patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:941-966 (doCancel, ServerClearAllActions at 965); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:1437 (combat hand-off clear); patch_manager_mods/companion_selfheal_src/CompanionSelfHeal.cpp:785-790 (pending end, TIMEOUT WARN at 789); patch_manager_mods/_shared_src/SelfHealUi.h:417, 435-443 (6000 ms unqueued stretch, past window)
- Claim: Cancel and the hand-off clear call ServerClearAllActions on the whole member creature, which also removes selfheal's 0x3F and round node. selfheal keeps g_pend.on and g_auto.pending set until 6 s of continuous unqueued time, then logs TIMEOUT unqueued as a WARN with no cause. The resting 0x3D is also cleared. Nothing in queue-hud re-adds it; whether the engine does is unverified.
- Scenario: companion 1 is at 40% with a selfheal heal queued. The user clicks Cancel on companion 1's strip to drop its own buffered power. The heal disappears silently, selfheal's auto stays blocked for about 6 s, then logs TIMEOUT unqueued. If HP is still low when the block ends and the cooldown has passed, auto re-issues the heal, undoing the user's cancel.
- Fix: doCancel should remove only queue-hud's own engine entries, or queue-hud should record the foreign wipe so selfheal ends its pending at once and logs the real cause.
- Confidence: med (the wipe and the 6 s stretch are verified in code; whether the engine re-adds 0x3D and whether auto actually re-fires are not verified).

## H10-F3 [med] edge-case
- Where: patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:1779-1788 (actionInQueue counts any 0x3F or round subtype 9/10, any owner); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHudLogic.h:1476-1477 (activeVerdict returns AV_PRESENT first, past window); QueueHudLogic.h:885-886 (DS_ACTIVE blocks dispatch, past window)
- Claim: queue-hud cannot tell its own 0x3F from a foreign one. An in-flight action stays AV_PRESENT after it has executed, and activeVerdict has no time bound on AV_PRESENT, so the buffer blocks.
- Scenario: companion 1: queue-hud serverCast A starts. selfheal's route A 0x3F (B) lands behind the resting 0x3D. A executes, but B is still in the engine queue, so actionInQueue stays true, the verdict stays AV_PRESENT, and the next buffered command C for companion 1 waits in DS_ACTIVE. C moves only after B runs, which needs kickStarved's gates (round pending, cr+0x11AC set, aa8/ab8 quiet, unpaused).
- Fix: Count only 0x3F entries whose round node matches buf.activeAction, and add a bounded AV_PRESENT wait that logs a foreign-head line when it expires.
- Confidence: med (the code path is verified; the stall length depends on engine gates I did not verify; this extends the BG23 known limit).

## H10-F4 [med] edge-case
- Where: patch_manager_mods/companion_selfheal_src/CompanionSelfHeal.cpp:402-407 (dedupe reads the engine queue only); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHudLogic.h:817-822 (decideAction buffers whenever the engine is busy or buf.active, past window); patch_manager_mods/action_hotbar_src/ActionHotbar.cpp:624-656 (targetedUse, no check against selfheal)
- Claim: The three mods have no shared view of heals in flight. selfheal's dedupe ignores queue-hud's buffered uses that are not yet in the engine, and hotbar @lowhp never consults selfheal's pending or the engine.
- Scenario: the leader is walking (0x40 MoveToPoint in the engine, not a use) at 35% HP. The player presses a hotbar Medpac. The engine is busy, so queue-hud buffers it (decideAction). selfheal's leader auto fires route A: its dedupe sees no use queued, and shouldAutoUse passes on its own cooldown. Two medpacs are used on the leader.
- Fix: Give selfheal and hotbar one shared check of buffered uses plus the engine queue (queue-hud publishes a per-member buffered-use count), and have hotbar @lowhp skip a creature whose selfheal pending is on.
- Confidence: low (the buffer and dedupe paths are verified; the walking state is an assumed trigger and was not reproduced).

## H10-F5 [low] bug
- Where: patch_manager_mods/action_hotbar_src/ActionHotbar.cpp:605-617 (CanUseItem and the target are chosen at press time; PerformUseItem at 617); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:2330-2337 (buffer push, no re-check); QueueHud.cpp:1935-1952 (dispatch gate and dispatchAction, past window)

- Scenario: paused. The player presses hotbar Medpac @lowhp twice. The second press buffers with companion 2 (40% HP) as target. Before dispatch selfheal heals companion 2 to full, or companion 2 dies. Dispatch still calls PerformUseItem on that target. The repo does not establish whether the engine then consumes the potion or refuses it.

- Confidence: low (the missing re-check is verified by absence; the engine's dispatch-time behaviour is not).

## H10-F6 [low] edge-case
- Where: patch_manager_mods/_shared_src/ActQueue.h:52-53 (bare 0x3F leftover stays behind 0x3D) and :58-66 (starved uses the first 0x3F); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHudLogic.h:1148-1155 (castStarvedAt takes the first 0x3F); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:1153-1190 (kickStarved: no give-up, 250 ms gate); patch_manager_mods/companion_selfheal_src/CompanionSelfHeal.cpp:867 (kick target = first 0x3F, past window)
- Claim: Both kick paths rotate toward the FIRST 0x3F. A bare leftover 0x3F, which the code says stays behind 0x3D, can be that first entry, so the kick brings the leftover forward instead of the real cast. queue-hud's kick has no give-up (selfheal has one at 5 s, CompanionSelfHeal.cpp:844-851), so a kick that cannot clear the head repeats every 250 ms.
- Scenario: queue [0x3D, 0x3F leftover (no round node), 0x3F real cast with a round node]. castStarvedAt returns 1 because the real cast's round node is pending. The kick rotates the leftover to the head and the real cast stays behind 0x3D. If the engine does not consume a headless 0x3F, the same state recurs and kickStarved logs SRV_KICK every 250 ms with no end.
- Fix: Pick the kick target as the first 0x3F that has a round node (or matches the active cmdId), and give kickStarved the same 5 s give-up selfheal uses.
- Confidence: low (the leftover comment and the missing give-up are in the code; the engine's handling of a headless 0x3F is not verified).

## H10-F7 [low] ux
- Where: patch_manager_mods/action_hotbar_src/ActionHotbar.cpp:513-514 (invoke logged as returned); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:2337 and 2343 (detour returns 1 when buffered or dropped); patch_manager_mods/companion_selfheal_src/CompanionSelfHeal.cpp:789 (TIMEOUT WARN, no cause)
- Claim: Each mod logs only its own view. Hotbar logs "invoke: returned" even when queue-hud consumed the call, selfheal's TIMEOUT WARN does not say another mod wiped its use, and no line links a queue-hud CANCEL or SRV_KICK to a selfheal use.
- Scenario: a hotbar press is buffered behind a busy queue. The hotbar log shows a normal return. A later drop or timeout appears with no shared cmdId, so a log review cannot tell which mod acted.
- Fix: Put queue-hud's buffered/dispatched verdict in the hotbar log (or share the cmdId), and tag selfheal's TIMEOUT line with the last queue-hud cmdId on that creature.
- Confidence: med (the paths are verified in code; no live log was read, per the log-access rule).

## H10-F8 [low] doc-mismatch
- Where: patch_manager_mods/_shared_src/GameAddr.h:90 (named UseItemRouteA, "called only by companion-selfheal"); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:84, 89 (same address used as AddUseTalentOnObject for spells); patch_manager_mods/companion_selfheal_src/common_contract_test.cpp:123 (pins the "Steam route A" name)
- Claim: The shared constant is documented as selfheal's item path only, but queue-hud's QL20 spell path calls the same 0x57D8B0. Both callers pass arguments matching the engine doc (talentType 0 = spell/item, patch_manager_mods/27_companion_selfheal_evidence/R1_actor_route/FINDINGS.md:11), so there is no ABI bug, only misleading naming.
- Scenario: a future edit treats the shared constant as item-only (for example, forces arg2 to 0) and silently breaks queue-hud's spell serverCast.
- Fix: Rename the constant to AddUseTalentOnObject in GameAddr.h, correct the comment, and update the contract test name.
- Confidence: high (addresses, comments, and argument layouts checked against the engine doc).

## H10-F9 [low] edge-case
- Where: patch_manager_mods/companion_selfheal_src/CompanionSelfHeal.cpp:821-822 (kickTick, no combat check) and :1160 (called from uiFrame before gateOpen at :1163); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:1685-1689 (queue-hud returns before kick and dispatch in combat)
- Claim: In combat only selfheal rotates companion queues, since queue-hud's kick is combat-gated and selfheal's is not. A rotation during a queue-hud hand-off is not checked against hand-off state.
- Scenario: combat starts while a queue-hud hand-off is in flight for companion 1 and selfheal has a pending. A starved selfheal 0x3F is rotated during the hand-off. Actual impact is unverified.
- Fix: Gate selfheal's kickTick on the same combat state queue-hud uses, or skip it while queue-hud's hand-off for that member is pending.
- Confidence: low (the kick and gate paths are verified; whether the HUD gate closes in combat, and how sensitive the hand-off is to a rotation, are not verified).
