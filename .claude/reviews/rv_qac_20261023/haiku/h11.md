## H11-F1 [high] bug
- Where: patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHud.cpp:82 (PerformCastSpellFn typedef), :1113 and :1121-1129 (replay passes a.targetId as 3rd arg, pp as 7th), :2373 (hook reads 3rd arg as targetId), :2379-2382 (7th arg read as a pointer and dereferenced), :2430-2431 (capture: posPtr ? posVal : nullptr); patch_manager_mods/queue_hud_src/QueueHudLogic.h:626-637 (makeSpellAction stores targetId and the pos copy), :650-652 (replayPos); patch_manager_mods/07_force_power_hotbar_evidence/target_modes/FINDINGS.md:9 (Q3 signature); patch_manager_mods/07_force_power_hotbar_evidence/target_modes/disasm_899270.txt:69 and :77 (vanilla pushes LEA EDX,[EBP-0x10] as the 3rd arg, then CALL 0x775700)
- Claim: The 3rd argument of PerformCastSpell is a float* pos, not a target id, and the 7th is the int flag. The hook and replay treat them the other way round. Queue-hud keeps the click-time 3rd argument, a stack address in the caller's frame, and passes it back at dispatch after that frame is gone. The 7th argument is also dereferenced as a float pointer.
- Scenario: Leader is paused and a Force power is queued from the hotbar or radial menu. The capture stores the stack address of the caller's local. After unpause, dispatch calls 0x775700 with that dead address as pos. The engine can read three reused stack floats, so a location-targeted power may go to a garbage position. Object-target casts use the same call. spell_replay_pos (QueueHudLogic.h:650-652) keys only on the 7th slot, so it never touches the pos slot.
- Fix: At capture, read the 3rd argument as a float* and copy its three floats (the 7th already gets this treatment). Replay with the copy in the 3rd slot and the captured int flag in the 7th, with the 2nd argument as the target id. Correct the typedef at QueueHud.cpp:82 to match Q3.
- Confidence: high on the slot mapping (Q3 plus disasm lines 69 and 77); med on impact (the engine's use of pos on object-target casts and the garbage read were not observed live). Read past the window at QueueHud.cpp:1113-1129 and 2366-2392 to follow the call.
