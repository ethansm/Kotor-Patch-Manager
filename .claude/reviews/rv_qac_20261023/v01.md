- Fix: In the tick, when buf.active and the creature has NO 0x3F/0x0F/0x2E entry and no real cur (creal false) but a 9/10 round node, accrue unpaused "orphan" time (reuse the staleCurSince pattern, same 1500 ms constant). ## V1-2 [med] PLAUSIBLE bug
- Sources: H2-F4 (restated; Haiku's "displaced click is untracked" is by design, the consequence below is the real defect)
- Where: QH:2317-2321 and QH:2416-2420 (in combat decideAction returns DISPATCH_NOW, hook sets active=true and overwrites activeAction); QL:818; QH:1684-1688 (tickItemQueues returns early in combat); QH:1342 (only the combat edge clears active); QL:1475-1486 (verdict at combat end)
- Defect: Every in-combat click leaves buf.active=true with activeStartTick = click time, but the buffer is not judged until combat ends (so sawExecuting/notePresent never latch). The last in-combat click therefore reaches the first non-combat tick as an active action of unknown history. Outcomes: unpaused and absent -> UNSEEN resolve after ~150 ms (harmless); but (a) if the player pauses within that window, rule 3 is skipped (ev.paused), rule 4b latches eaten, and after unpause rule 5/8 (idle engine) gives AV_EATEN_REINJECT: the already-executed combat power/item is sent again; (b) for a member k>0 (leader switched during combat) with a server-eligible Force power, hasFollowLeader is true at rest, the action is absent and sawExecuting false, so the RI_OK branch (QH:1824) re-casts it immediately (up to 2 times, see V1-3). Each in-combat click also overwrites the previous activeAction without a RESOLVE/DROP line, so the trace cannot show what became of it.
- Scenario: In combat the leader casts Force Push (click, active=true). Fight ends; player switches leader and the old leader is a companion at rest with 0x3D: Force Push is re-sent at the dead target (or paused variant: re-sent on unpause).
- Fix: Do not keep tracking pass-through clicks: in the hook DISPATCH_NOW branch when `g_combat != 0`, do not set buf.active (or set it only when `!g_combatUnreadable` and call a new `endAction`-style RESOLVE reason "combat_passthru"). Alternatively at the first non-combat tick after combat (edge in tickItemQueues) call endAction on any active whose activeStartTick predates the combat end, logging RESOLVE seen=0 why=combat_end. Log a DROP/OVERWRITE line when the hook replaces a still-active cmdId.
- Test: QT: a decideAction/hook-level helper `trackInCombat(...)` is not pure today; add a pure `shouldTrackAfterCombat(activeStartTick, combatEndTick)` and vectors (before end -> false, after end -> true). PLAY row: fight, cast a power twice in combat, end combat while unpaused then immediately pause (space) at once; pass signal: no `re-injecting eaten` line and no second CMD for the combat spell after unpause.
- Engine question: Does the client Perform* wrapper pass the controlled creature pointer, so memberK is 0 in combat? (if yes, only the paused variant (a) applies, with a ~150 ms window; variant (b) needs a leader switch during combat.)

## V1-4 [med] PLAUSIBLE bad-assumption
- Sources: H2-F6, H6-F7, H12-F3
- Where: QL:1505-1518 (makeEngineView: ev.executing = any 0x2E/0x0F entry || unpaused creal 9/10 || aa8 != 0 || ab8 != 0); QL:776-778 (notePresent latches sawExecuting on every AV_PRESENT frame); QH:1855 (call site); QL:1480 (rule 2 AV_EXECUTED, no re-inject); QL:1256-area comment (aa8/ab8 count as exec by design)
- Defect: "executing" is read from creature-level signals with no check that the node/flag belongs to our action. During the first 650 ms the verdict is always AV_PRESENT (rules 1-2 at QL:1478-1479), so any foreign 0x2E/0x0F entry or a flag left from the previous command latches sawExecuting. An action that is then wiped (or never started) resolves AV_EXECUTED: no re-inject, no DROP, command lost. Two reachable inputs: (1) dispatch under `flag_cap` (QL:842/859, 2000 ms) while ab8 >= 1 from the previous cast (ab8 stays until EndCombatRound, PQ2 3.3 ~3000 ms) sets executing on the first frame; (2) the freshly expanded 0xF/0x2E node (spell conj 170 ms, or a long walk-to-range for a Force power, PQ2 5.2 `0xA5ED` out of range) counts as executing before the spell fires, so a wipe in that window reads as EXECUTED.
- Scenario: Cast A, then B is dispatched at the 2 s flag cap while A's round flags are still set. A save/BumpFriends wipe eats B. sawExecuting was latched by A's ab8, so B resolves EXECUTED and is never cast.
- Fix: Latch sawExecuting only for evidence tied to the active action: a 0xF/0x2E entry whose param0 equals the active spellId/clientItem, or cur subType 9/10 with matching id, or aa8/ab8 rising from 0 after markDispatched (store the flags' value at dispatch and require a change or require them to have been 0 at dispatch). Keep "expanded but unfired" as its own state (eaten-eligible).
- Test: QT: makeEngineView/notePresent vectors: ab8=1 at dispatch and unchanged -> executing false; 0xF with other spell id -> false; matching id -> true; flag 0->1 after dispatch -> true. PLAY row: two Force powers back-to-back with dispatch_wait_flags cap reached; pass signal: VANISH kind and RESOLVE seen= values consistent with both casts landing.
- Engine question: Does the 0xF/0x2E node leave the queue at fire (so a wipe between expansion and fire exists), and how often does the 2 s flag cap trigger in play (ab8 can be set ~3 s)?
