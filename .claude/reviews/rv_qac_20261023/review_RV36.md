## R2 [BLOCKING] plan_RV3 §RQ8 (restoreHead can resurrect a command the player just cancelled)
Problem: the restore runs for `h.pending && headValid && !headHeld` at combat end. The vanilla Cancel Combat button (BTN_CLEARALL) ends combat in the same press (R7 sec 2: `0x77FD20` clears combat mode, then ClearAllActions). doCancel and the vanilla button do NOT end the hand-off or clear `h.head` (doCancel only clears the buffer, QueueHud.cpp:963; the cancel_click drop exists only inside the `headHeld` branch, :1380-1393). That is a double-use of a consumable the player explicitly cancelled.
Second gap: with RV1 EDGE_WAIT and `!inEngine` the plan sets `headHeld = true` plus `h.waiting`; RQ8 then returns RH_NONE for it and the head is silently dropped, contradicting the plan's "nothing else to do".
Evidence: QueueHud.cpp:963-966, 1380-1393, 1740-1742; plan_RV1.md:182-195; plan_RV3.md:59.
Required correction: in the RQ8 loop add, before `restoreHead`:
